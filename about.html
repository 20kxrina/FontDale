Our Story

2023

The Concept

Envisioned by Franz Diego, FontDale was conceived not as a mall, but as an architectural poem, a seamless sanctuary connecting modern urban spaces with timeless style.

2025

The Realization

Opened in 2025, FontDale stands as a premier destination for global fashion, elite lifestyle, and diverse dining within its two main wings: The North Gallery and The South Pavilion.

The Signature Connectivity

The Lumen Skywalk

At the heart of our contemporary layout, the signature Lumen Skywalk, a grand glass architectural marvel, bridges our two wings, inviting visitors to seamlessly explore the elegance of FontDale.
